TCEA - AttendHub

AttendHub is a smart web-based attendance management system specially designed for students to record attendance when the teacher is absent. In many situations, classes continue even without the teacher, and maintaining attendance manually can be difficult or unorganized. AttendHub provides a simple and reliable digital solution to this problem.
